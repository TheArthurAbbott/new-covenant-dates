# Research: Start Dates for Daniel's 70 Weeks

Daniel’s 70 weeks prophecy (Daniel 9:24–27) is analyzed across different interpretations and starting points. Below are the possible date ranges for the prophecy's beginning, supported by references to Biblical decrees and events.

---

## 1. **Jeremiah’s Prophecy**
- **Scripture Reference:** Jeremiah 25:11–12; 29:10
- **Date Range:** Approx. 605–536 BC
  - **Context:** Jeremiah predicted a 70-year exile under Babylon until the Lord restored His people.
  - **Significance:** This spans from Babylon’s rise to power (605 BC) to the first return under Persian King Cyrus (~536 BC). The exile is seen as a possible prelude to Daniel’s prophecy.

---

## 2. **The Decree of Cyrus**
- **Scripture Reference:** Ezra 1:1–4
- **Date Range:** 538–537 BC
  - **Context:** King Cyrus issued a decree allowing the Jews to return to Jerusalem to rebuild the temple.
  - **Significance:** Some view this as fulfilling Jeremiah’s prophecy, though it focused on temple reconstruction rather than the city.

---

## 3. **The Decree of Darius I**
- **Scripture Reference:** Ezra 6:1–12
- **Date Range:** ~520 BC
  - **Context:** Darius reaffirmed Cyrus’s decree, ensuring progress continued on temple work amidst opposition.
  - **Significance:** Like Cyrus's decree, this emphasizes temple work rather than city restoration.

---

## 4. **The Decree of Artaxerxes I (Ezra)**
- **Scripture Reference:** Ezra 7:11–26
- **Date Range:** 458–457 BC
  - **Context:** Artaxerxes provided Ezra resources and jurisdiction over religious and civil affairs.
  - **Significance:** While civil and judicial authority was expanded, direct city rebuilding is not explicitly mentioned.

---

## 5. **The Decree of Artaxerxes I (Nehemiah)**
- **Scripture Reference:** Nehemiah 2:1–8
- **Date Range:** 445–444 BC
  - **Context:** Artaxerxes authorized Nehemiah to rebuild Jerusalem’s walls and infrastructure.
  - **Significance:** Many interpret this as the definitive fulfillment of “restore and rebuild Jerusalem” in Daniel 9:25.

---

## Summary Table

| Starting Point                             | Approximate Date         |
|--------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Jeremiah’s 70 years (Babylonian exile)     | 605–536 BC              |
| Cyrus’s decree (for the temple)            | 538–537 BC              |
| Darius’s decree (for the temple)           | ~520 BC                 |
| Artaxerxes to Ezra (temple/authority)      | 458–457 BC              |
| Artaxerxes to Nehemiah (walls/city)        | 445–444 BC              |

---

## Notes

1. Jeremiah’s prophecy addresses the entire exile period, focusing on Israel’s release from captivity[1].
2. Cyrus’s decree emphasizes temple reconstruction, regarded by some as initiating Daniel's timeline[2].
3. Darius’s reaffirmation of Cyrus’s decree provided continuity; its relevance to Jerusalem’s city remains debated[3].
4. Artaxerxes’s support for Ezra may signify gradual restoration authority extending to Jerusalem governance[4].
5. Nehemiah’s commission reflects the most explicit rebuilding of Jerusalem’s infrastructure, aligning closely with Daniel’s wording[5].
