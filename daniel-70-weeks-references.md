# References for Daniel's 70 Weeks of Years

Below are reputable scholarly and religious resources analyzing the "70 weeks of years" prophecy in Daniel 9:24-27. These works encompass different perspectives, including critical, traditional, and dispensational views, among others. 

---

### Scholarly and Religious References

1. **Dr. Wendy Widder’s Lecture on Daniel 9:20-27**
   - Discusses perspectives from the Maccabean/Antiochian (2nd century BC) to Messianic and Eschatological interpretations.
   - [Read more](https://www.biblicalelearning.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Widder_Daniel_Session15.pdf).

2. **Stephen R. Miller, "Interpreting Daniel’s Seventy Weeks: Dismal Swamp or Blessed Hope?”**
   - Provides views on literal-futurist or symbolic analysis.
   - [Details here](https://www.biblemattersonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/The-Seventy-Weeks-of-Daniel-Journal-MABTS-Miller.pdf).

3. **G.P. Hugenberger, “The Seventy 'Weeks' Prophecy of Daniel 9”**
   - Compares critical, messianic, and dispensational schools of thought.
   - [Study Overview](https://www.parkstreet.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/70weeks_danielprophecy.pdf).

4. **Jacques Doukhan, "The Seventy Weeks of Daniel 9: An Exegetical Study"**
   - Explores chronological interpretations and textual challenges.
   - [Read more](https://www.andrews.edu/library/car/cardigital/Periodicals/AUSS/1979-1/1979-1-01.pdf).

5. **Re-Examining and Re-Interpreting the 70 Weeks Prophecy (Academic Thesis)**
   - A recent thesis exploring alternative interpretations linked to biblical figures and events.
   - [View document](https://www.academia.edu/84018757/RE_EXAMINING_AND_RE_INTERPRETING_THE_70_WEEKS_PROPHECY_A_THESIS_Dan_9_25_27).

---

These references provide further insights into the historical, theological, and literary dimensions of Daniel's prophecy, positioning it within various interpretative frameworks.
