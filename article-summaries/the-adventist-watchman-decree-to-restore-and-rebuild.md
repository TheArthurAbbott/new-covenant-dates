# The Decree to Restore and Build Jerusalem & How Prophetic Time Is Calculated

Both videos argue that Daniel 9:25’s starting point must be the **457 BC Artaxerxes decree**, and that prophetic time uses **360‑day units for calculation** but **solar/seasonal years for fulfillment**.  
Together, they form a unified case for the Adventist timeline of the 70 Weeks and 2300 Days.

---

### Which Decree Restored and Built Jerusalem?

The video argues that the decree of **Artaxerxes in 457 BC** is the only decree that fits both the **chronological** and **legal** requirements of Daniel 9:25.  
It reframes “going forth” (moza) as the **effective implementation** of the decree when Ezra arrived in Jerusalem.

### Does Prophetic Time Use a 360‑Day or 365‑Day Year?

The second video argues that prophetic **time units** use a 360‑day standard (e.g., 1260 days = time, times, half a time), but **prophetic fulfillment** uses real-world **season‑to‑season years**.  
It uses the 40‑year wilderness wandering to show that God counts **actual years**, not 360‑day cycles.

## Events

Together, the videos trace the decrees of Ezra–Nehemiah and the mechanics of prophetic time to argue that **457 BC** is the only viable starting point for Daniel’s 70 Weeks and 2300 Days.

---

### Cyrus’ First Decree: 538 BC

> “If you calculate 483 years from 538 BC, you come out to about 55 BC.” (4:41–5:04)

This is far too early to reach the Messiah, so it is excluded on timing alone.

---

### Darius’ Decree: 520 BC

> “This will take us to about the year 37 BC.” (5:13–5:30)

Still too early to reach Christ’s ministry, so it is also excluded.

---

### Artaxerxes’ Decree (Ezra 7): 457 BC

> “It takes us to about the year 27 AD.” (5:59–6:06)

This is the only decree whose 483‑year span reaches the time of Christ.  
The video argues that “going forth” (moza) refers to the **results** of the decree when Ezra implemented it in Jerusalem.

---

### Nehemiah’s Authorization: 444/445 BC

> “It takes us to about the year 40 AD.” (6:31–6:52)

Too late to reach the Messiah.  
Additionally, Nehemiah received **permission**, not a **decree**, and it concerned **walls**, not the city.

---

### Wilderness Wandering: 1446–1406 BC (40 Years)

> “Moses was 120 years old when he died… Aaron was 123.” (21:01–21:33)

The video argues that Moses and Aaron’s synchronized ages prove the 40 years were **actual solar years**, not 40 cycles of 360 days.

## Key Concepts

Together, the videos present a unified interpretive framework:  
**(1)** The decree must fit the **chronology**, **(2)** the decree must fit the **legal context**, and **(3)** prophetic time uses **360‑day units** but **365‑day fulfillment**.

---

### The “Going Forth” (Moza) Principle

- **Premise 1:** Daniel 9:25 uses *moza*, meaning “result” or “product.”  
- **Premise 2:** Ezra 7’s decree only “goes forth” when Ezra arrives and implements it.  
- **Conclusion:** The decree’s effective date is **autumn 457 BC**, not the spring date it was written.

**Commentary:** This is a linguistic argument hinging on the noun *moza*; it is internally coherent but depends on accepting Ellen White’s framing and the narrative reconstruction of Ezra–Nehemiah.

---

### Prophetic Time Unit vs. Prophetic Time Application

- **Premise 1:** Prophetic units (time, times, half a time) use a **360‑day standard**.  
- **Premise 2:** Actual fulfillment uses **real-world seasonal years** (spring to spring).  
- **Conclusion:** The 70 Weeks and 2300 Days should be calculated using **solar years**, not 360‑day cycles.

**Commentary:** This distinction allows Adventist timelines to maintain both the 360‑day symbolism and the 1844 fulfillment without contradiction.
